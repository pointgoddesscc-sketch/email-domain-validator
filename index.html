About the Original Research

This tool was inspired by research into the sample email caneiro@interestingsite.com. That address (and the domain interestingsite.com) is a well-known placeholder used in educational invoices, Microsoft-style templates, résumés, and training documents. The domain itself is currently parked and listed for sale.

Use this tool to keep your business data clean and your marketing lists accurate.
